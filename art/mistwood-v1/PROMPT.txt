雾林温室 / Mistwood Conservatory · 场景固定设计

A grand realistic forest conservatory sanctuary on a broad floating limestone cliff island. Ancient irregular branching trees frame the rear left, an ornate weathered iron and glass Victorian conservatory stands on a stone plinth to the rear right. A broad gently arched limestone bridge crosses a shallow river that spills from both sides into long silver waterfalls. Clear central moss and flagstone courtyard for a small silver tabby cat to run and play. Weathered masonry, fern beds, grasses, rocks, timber benches, warm stone lanterns, understated natural materials. Distant cinematic mountain forest and valley mist, believable architectural scale and subdued photoreal color. Preserve clear walkable banks, wide bridge approaches and sightlines to the cat. No cartoon candy colors, no spherical tree canopies.
